The base rate Build a target list of 7–10 PIs, not one. Silence and rejection are the normal response, not a verdict on you. “No current openings” is a scheduling fact about this semester — ask again at the next semester boundary.

Working backwards from where you want to end up

Pick the institution, then find who at UF already has the thread

Most advice tells you to find a lab. This does the reverse: name the place you want to be in five years, then work out whether anyone at UF has a real connection to it. Thirty of the forty-five institutions on our map have a named person behind them, and that person is a warm introduction rather than a cold application. The rest are honest about having nothing.

The one region where none of this helps No Chinese university appears anywhere in UF's exchange list, and no UF faculty member on this map trained at one. If your target is Tsinghua, Peking or USTC, there is no thread to pull and no introduction to be had. That route runs through direct application instead — the Chinese Government Scholarship, Schwarzman or Yenching — and it is set out in full on the study abroad page.
